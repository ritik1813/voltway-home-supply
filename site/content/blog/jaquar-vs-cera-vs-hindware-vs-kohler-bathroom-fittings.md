Bathroom fittings are the most visible plumbing purchase and the one where brand opinions run strongest. Jaquar, Cera, Hindware and Kohler between them fit most bathrooms in Gurgaon, and each has a place. The right choice depends less on which brand is "best" and more on your budget per bathroom, how easily you can get a spare cartridge in five years and one decision that has to be made before the tiles go on. Here is how we see them.

## Taps and showers

Most of what you pay for in a tap you cannot see. The points that matter:

- **Body.** A good tap has a heavy brass body. Weight in the hand is a fair first test; very light taps use thinner castings or other alloys.
- **Cartridge.** Modern taps use a ceramic disc cartridge. It is the part that eventually wears, so its availability years later is what "spares" really means.
- **Plating.** Chrome over nickel, thick and even. Hard water tests plating at the base of the spout and around the handle first.
- **Aerator.** The small mesh at the spout that softens the flow. It clogs with scale and should unscrew easily for cleaning.

**Jaquar** is the default in Gurgaon for a reason: the widest range at each price, concealed diverters and thermostats in most collections, and service that is close at hand. **Kohler** sits above it on finish, design and price. **Cera** and **Hindware** make sound taps at lower prices and are sensible where the budget is tight or the bathroom is not the main one. **Grohe**, the German brand, competes with Kohler at the premium end and is usually chosen because a designer has specified it.

## WCs and basins

Here the order changes. Hindware and Cera made their names in sanitaryware and offer very good value in wall-hung and floor-mounted WCs and in basins. Jaquar and Kohler make excellent sanitaryware too, at higher prices, and are usually chosen to match their taps.

Whichever brand you choose, check:

- **the trap type and distance.** For a floor-mounted WC with a floor outlet, measure from the finished wall to the centre of the outlet, commonly 100, 225 or 300 mm, and buy a WC to match. This single measurement causes more returns than anything else in the bathroom;
- **rimless bowl and soft-close seat,** both worth having;
- **the seat cover model,** because seats are specific to the WC and are the first part to need replacing;
- **the concealed cistern and frame** for a wall-hung WC, which is a separate item with its own brand decision.

Mixing brands between taps and sanitaryware is normal and often the best value: Jaquar taps with Hindware or Cera sanitaryware is a common Gurgaon combination.

## The decision you must make before tiling

Wall mixers, diverters and thermostatic showers have a **concealed body** that is fixed inside the wall before tiling, and a visible trim that goes on afterwards. The body of one brand accepts only that brand's trim, and often only certain collections within it. So the brand of your shower fittings is decided on the day the plumber fixes the concealed bodies, weeks before you choose anything else.

Decide the tap brand and collection first, buy the concealed bodies with the pipes, and buy the trims later. Our [bathroom renovation checklist](/blog/bathroom-renovation-plumbing-material-checklist/) separates the two stages item by item.

## Which is cheaper?

Roughly, from lowest to highest for a comparable bathroom: the economy lines of Hindware and Cera and Jaquar's Essco; then mainstream Cera and Hindware; then Jaquar; then Kohler and Grohe; then the luxury lines such as Artize. Within a brand, the collection matters as much as the brand: an entry Jaquar collection and a premium one can differ by several times.

A useful way to budget is per bathroom rather than per piece. Decide a figure for each bathroom, spend more on the main one and the guest one, and choose a simpler collection of the same brand for the others so spares stay common.

## Hard water in Gurgaon

Water in much of Gurgaon is hard, and scale is what ages fittings here. Whatever the brand:

- choose showers with rubber nozzles that can be rubbed clean;
- unscrew and clean aerators every few months;
- wipe taps dry rather than scrubbing with acid cleaners, which strip plating;
- matt black and coloured finishes show scale more than chrome and need more care;
- fit an angle valve at every point so one tap can be serviced without shutting the flat.

## Warranty and genuine product

All four give long warranties on taps and sanitaryware, with shorter terms on cartridges, seats and electronic parts. The period differs by brand and collection, so read the card in the box. A claim needs an invoice from an authorised dealer; a cash memo from a counter is not enough.

Copies of popular tap designs are common in NCR markets. Genuine pieces come in sealed, branded boxes with the model code, MRP label and warranty card, and the model code on the box matches the invoice.

## Which to choose

- **Rental flat or tight budget:** Cera or Hindware throughout, or Essco taps with Hindware or Cera sanitaryware.
- **Your own flat, sensible budget:** Jaquar taps and showers in a mid collection, with Jaquar, Hindware or Cera sanitaryware. This is where most Gurgaon bathrooms land.
- **Premium bathroom:** Kohler or Grohe, or Jaquar's upper collections or Artize. Choose on the design, then confirm the concealed bodies and every accessory are available.
- **Project or multi-tower:** follow the approved-makes list, and pick the make whose service team will support handover snags across phases.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which is better, Jaquar or Cera?

For taps and showers, Jaquar has the wider range, more concealed options and the easiest spares in Gurgaon, at a higher price. Cera offers good value, particularly in WCs and basins. Many homes use Jaquar taps with Cera or Hindware sanitaryware.

### Is Hindware better than Jaquar for sanitaryware?

Hindware is one of India's oldest sanitaryware makers and gives very good value in WCs and basins. Jaquar sanitaryware is also good and usually costs more; it is often chosen to match Jaquar taps. Either is a sound choice if the trap type and distance suit your bathroom.

### Is Kohler worth the extra cost over Jaquar?

Kohler sits higher on finish and design, and that is what the extra money buys. In daily use and durability a mid-range Jaquar fitting installed properly will also last for many years. Choose Kohler where the look of the bathroom matters most to you.

### Which bathroom fittings brand is cheapest?

Among known brands, the economy lines of Hindware and Cera and Jaquar's Essco are the least expensive. They are the safe way to save money. Avoid unbranded copies of popular designs: the plating and cartridges fail early and no spares exist.

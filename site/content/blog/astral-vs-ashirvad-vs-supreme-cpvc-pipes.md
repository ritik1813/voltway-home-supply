Once you have decided on CPVC for the hot and cold lines, the plumber's next question is which make. In Gurgaon the answer is nearly always Astral, Ashirvad or Supreme. All three are ISI-marked, all three will outlast the bathroom tiles if they are installed properly, and the difference between them is smaller than the difference between a good joint and a bad one. Here is how they compare and what actually decides whether the pipe in your wall stays dry.

## What is the same

All three are made to IS 15778, the Indian standard for CPVC pipes for hot and cold water. That standard fixes the outside diameter, the wall thickness for each class and the pressure the pipe must hold. So in every one of the three:

- the pipe is rated for hot water up to about 93 °C;
- it comes in two classes, **SDR 11** (thicker wall, for hot lines) and **SDR 13.5** (for cold lines);
- sizes run from 15 mm to 50 mm for domestic work, in 3 metre and 5 metre lengths;
- joints are made with CPVC solvent cement.

## What actually differs

**The compound.** CPVC pipe is extruded from a compound, and brands source or formulate it differently. Ashirvad makes its pipe under licence from Lubrizol, the American company behind the FlowGuard Plus compound, and prints that name on the pipe. Prince and Finolex sell FlowGuard Plus pipe on the same basis. Astral and Supreme sell under their own range names. In a flat, at domestic pressure and geyser temperature, you will not see a difference in service between them.

**Fittings.** This is where a plumber forms a preference. The fit of a pipe in a socket, the depth of the socket and the quality of the brass inserts vary slightly by brand, and a plumber who has used one make for years knows exactly how its joints behave. That familiarity is worth more than a small price difference.

**Availability of the odd fitting.** Every job runs short of two elbows and a reducer on the last day. Astral is the easiest to top up anywhere in NCR; Ashirvad and Supreme are widely available through their dealers. If your site is far from a market, order ten percent extra fittings at the start.

**The rest of the system.** Supreme and Astral both make the UPVC, SWR drainage and tank fittings you will also need, so one brand can cover the whole plumbing list.

## Which is cheaper?

Supreme is usually a little below Astral and Ashirvad, which sit close to each other. Prince and Finolex are typically in the same band as Supreme or just under it. On a complete flat the gap between the cheapest and the dearest of these is a small part of the plumbing bill, because the pipe itself is not where the money goes; fittings, brass transitions and labour are. Choosing a make to save a few percent, and then mixing in another make's fittings because they were to hand, is the false economy.

## Do not mix brands

Because all makes follow the same standard, one brand's pipe will go into another brand's fitting. Plumbers do it all the time. We advise against it for three reasons:

1. **Tolerances.** The standard allows a small range on diameters. A pipe at one end of the range in a fitting at the other gives a joint that is either too tight to seat fully or too loose to bond well.
2. **Solvent cement.** Each brand's cement is formulated and tested with its own pipe. Use the same brand of pipe, fittings and cement.
3. **Warranty.** No brand will entertain a complaint on a mixed system.

One make for pipe, fittings and solvent cement, throughout the flat.

## How to check a genuine pipe

- **Printing along the pipe:** brand, range name, size, class (SDR 11 or 13.5), the standard and the ISI mark with licence number, repeated at regular intervals and sharp.
- **Colour and finish:** even colour, smooth inside and out, cut ends clean without a grey or chalky core.
- **Packaging:** fittings in printed, sealed bags; holograms or QR codes where the brand uses them.
- **Invoice:** a GST invoice from an authorised dealer with the brand and class named. A bill that says only "CPVC pipe 20 mm" protects nobody.

Counterfeits are less common than in wire, but a more frequent problem is substitution: SDR 13.5 supplied where SDR 11 was ordered, or a cheaper make sent on a top-up. Check the printing on delivery.

## Installation matters more than the brand

Most complaints that reach us as "the pipe failed" turn out to be joints. Whichever make you buy:

- cut square, deburr and clean the pipe end;
- apply CPVC solvent cement to both the pipe and the fitting, push home with a quarter turn and hold;
- allow the cure time printed on the tin before filling the line;
- use brass-insert fittings at every threaded connection and do not over-tighten;
- pressure test for 24 hours before the tiler starts;
- protect pipes on the terrace from direct sun with paint or insulation, and insulate long hot runs.

## Which to choose

- **Flat owner renovating a bathroom or kitchen:** the make your plumber knows best among the three, SDR 11 on hot lines. If the plumber has no preference, Astral or Ashirvad.
- **Builder floor or independent house:** any of the three; Supreme is good value when the quantity is larger and you are buying UPVC and drainage from the same brand.
- **Project or multi-tower:** follow the approved-makes list. Astral and Ashirvad are on most of them, Supreme and Prince on many. Pick the make your supplier can deliver in every size and fitting across phases.
- **Adding to existing plumbing:** stay with the make already in the wall where you can identify it.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which is better, Astral or Ashirvad CPVC?

Both are premium, ISI-marked and accepted on almost every project specification. There is no meaningful difference in service life in a flat. Choose the one your plumber has the most experience with, and buy the pipe, fittings and solvent cement in that one make.

### Is Supreme CPVC as good as Astral?

Yes for domestic plumbing. Supreme pipe is made to the same Indian standard, IS 15778, and is commonly approved on projects. It is usually priced a little lower, which makes it good value on larger quantities.

### Which CPVC pipe brand is cheapest?

Among the well-known makes, Supreme, Prince and Finolex are usually a little below Astral and Ashirvad. The difference on a single flat is small. Avoid unbranded or unmarked pipe at any price; the cost of one leak inside a wall is far more than the saving.

### Can I use Astral pipe with Ashirvad or Supreme fittings?

It will physically fit, because all are made to the same standard, but it is not advisable. Small differences in tolerance affect the joint, the solvent cement is matched to its own brand and no manufacturer will support a mixed system. Keep to one make.

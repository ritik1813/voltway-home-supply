Every 3BHK list we quote in Gurgaon now carries the question: BLDC or normal? The short answer is that in a city where fans run from March to October, a BLDC fan pays back its premium in two to three years on a fan that runs most of the day, and never pays back on a fan that runs an hour a week. Here is the arithmetic and the practical differences.

## What BLDC means

A standard ceiling fan uses a single-phase induction motor with a capacitor. A BLDC (brushless DC) fan has a permanent-magnet motor driven by electronics inside the fan. The electronics convert mains AC to DC and switch the windings, which is far more efficient and allows precise speed control from a remote.

## Power consumption

| | Standard induction fan | BLDC fan |
|---|---|---|
| Power at full speed | 70 to 80 W | 26 to 35 W |
| Power at low speed (with electronic regulator) | 40 to 55 W | 6 to 12 W |
| Speed control | Wall regulator | Remote, often app |
| Typical warranty | 2 years | 2 to 5 years (motor) |
| Price premium | Base | 1.5 to 2.5 times a comparable standard fan |

The low-speed figure matters most. Induction fans waste a large share of their power as heat in the regulator and motor at low speed; BLDC fans draw almost nothing.

## Payback in a Gurgaon home

Assume a bedroom fan running 14 hours a day for 240 days a year, an honest figure for a Gurgaon summer and monsoon with the fan on through the night.

- Standard fan at an average of 65 W: 65 W × 14 h × 240 days = about 218 units a year.
- BLDC fan at an average of 28 W: 28 W × 14 h × 240 days = about 94 units a year.
- Saving: about 124 units per fan per year.

At a blended Haryana domestic tariff in the region of 6 to 7 rupees a unit (higher slabs cost more, and most 3BHKs with ACs sit in the higher slabs), that is roughly 750 to 900 rupees a year per fan. If the BLDC fan costs 2,000 to 3,000 rupees more than the equivalent standard fan, payback is two to four years, and the fan should last ten or more.

Multiply across five or six fans and a 3BHK saves something like 4,000 to 5,000 rupees a year, more if the flat is on inverter backup, because BLDC fans draw less than half the backup current and run far longer on the same battery.

For a guest-room fan used a few days a month, the saving is negligible and a good standard fan is the sensible buy.

## Practical differences

**Regulator.** BLDC fans do not use the wall regulator. The wall switch becomes on-off and speed is set from the remote. If you are renovating, tell the electrician so the regulator module is dropped from the switchboard (it saves a module). If you are retrofitting, leave the old regulator on full or have it removed; running a BLDC fan through a working regulator can damage the electronics.

**Remote dependence.** Losing the remote is the single most common complaint. Most brands now sell replacements and several offer app control or a wall-mounted remote holder.

**Inverter and voltage.** BLDC fans have built-in wide-voltage electronics and cope well with Gurgaon's voltage swings and inverter supply. Standard fans hum on some inverters.

**Noise and wobble.** Good BLDC fans are quieter because there is no regulator hum and no capacitor. Cheap ones can produce an electronic whine at low speed; stick to established brands.

**Air delivery.** Compare the CMM (cubic metres per minute) figure, not the wattage. Good BLDC fans match standard fans at 210 to 240 CMM for a 1200 mm sweep.

**Decorative and designer fans.** The premium fan segment has moved almost entirely to BLDC, so if you want a particular look you may not have a choice.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much electricity does a BLDC fan save?

Roughly 50 to 65 percent compared with a standard fan running the same hours, more at low speeds. For a fan running most of the day through a Gurgaon summer that is around 120 units a year per fan.

### Do BLDC fans work with a normal regulator?

No. They should be wired to a plain on-off switch and controlled by their remote or app. Running a BLDC fan through an electronic regulator can damage its circuit board.

### Can BLDC fans run on an inverter during power cuts?

Yes, and they are much better at it. A BLDC fan draws less than half the current of a standard fan, so the same inverter battery runs it two to three times longer.

### Are BLDC fans worth it for a rented flat?

Only if you plan to take them with you or stay several years. The payback is two to four years on heavily used fans. For a short tenancy, a good standard fan is the better buy.
